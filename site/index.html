What is Venu

One platform. Every event, its own.

Venu is a white-label event app platform. One codebase reskins entirely from a per-client config, so every organizer gets an experience that feels built just for them.

01

No app store

One QR scan drops the app on the home screen in under ten seconds. No download, no approval, no wall between your attendee and your event.

02

Total white-label

Your logo, your colors, your name across every screen — at your own address or your own domain. It's your app, not "an event app."

03

Built modular

Every feature is a module. Switch on what fits, pay for nothing you don't. Conference, gala, wedding, arena — one engine, infinite skins.

How it works

From QR code to full production.

1

We brand it

Your logo, colors, schedule, speakers, and sponsors go live at your own subdomain. We build the app around your event — you don't touch a line of code.

2

Attendees scan

One QR code on table tents, badges, signage, and stage slides. A scan adds the app to their home screen — no store, no account wall.

3

The event comes alive

Real-time schedule, networking, crowd photo wall on the main screens, live polls, sponsor spotlights — the whole room, connected.
