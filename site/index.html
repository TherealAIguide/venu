What is Venu

One platform. Every event, its own.

Venu is a white-label event platform. Every event is built around your brand, your attendees, and the moments that matter — not a template with your logo dropped on top.

01

No app store

One QR scan drops the app on the home screen in under ten seconds. No download, no approval, no wall between your attendee and your event.

02

Total white-label

Your logo, your colors, your name across every screen — curated for your attendees and your brand. It's your app, not "an event app."

03

Built modular

Every feature is a module. Switch on what fits, pay for nothing you don't. Conference, gala, wedding, arena — one engine, infinite possible experience combinations.

How it works

From QR code to full production.

1

Consult & curate

We sit down with you to shape the experience — your brand, your attendees, the moments that matter — then curate the right modules and build the app around your event. You don't touch a line of code.

2

Attendees scan

One QR code on table tents, badges, signage, and stage slides. A scan adds the app to their home screen — no store, no account wall.

3

The event comes alive

Real-time schedule, networking, crowd photo wall on the main screens, live polls, sponsor spotlights — the whole room, connected.
